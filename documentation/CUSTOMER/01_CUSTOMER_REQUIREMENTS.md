# PLATED – Software Requirements Specification: Role 04 Customer (Catering Services Discovery & Booking)
**Document Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Approved for Implementation  
**Role:** Catering Client / Event Host  
**Business Domain:** Catering Services Marketplace & Booking Management

---

## 1. Role Purpose & Definition

The **Customer** represents individuals, corporate event planners, wedding hosts, and organizations seeking professional catering services on PLATED. A customer account allows users to:
- Browse and discover verified catering companies in their city.
- Explore food menus, package options, and pricing structures.
- Submit structured catering inquiries with date, venue, guest count, and dietary preferences.
- Review and accept competitive quotations generated by caterers.
- Track event milestones and real-time status progressions (Inquiry $\to$ Quoted $\to$ Confirmed $\to$ In Progress $\to$ Completed).
- Access itemized milestone invoices and record deposits/payments securely.
- Submit post-event ratings and reviews to build marketplace reputation.

---

## 2. Functional Requirements

### FR-CUST-01: Registration & Authentication
- Self-registration via `/api/auth/customer/register` with full name, email, password, phone, city, delivery address, and preferred cuisines.
- Direct login via `/api/auth/customer/login` and unified `/api/auth/login`.
- Auto-provisioning of `CustomerProfile` linked 1:1 with `User` (`role = 'customer'`).

### FR-CUST-02: Catering Company & Menu Discovery
- Browse directory of approved catering businesses (`GET /api/customer/caterers`).
- Inspect catering company profile, business type, address, GST, and specialties.
- View caterer menus, categories, and item pricing (`GET /api/companies/{company_id}/menus/public`).
- Browse packages (`per_guest` or `flat_rate`).

### FR-CUST-03: Event Inquiry Submission
- Submit catering inquiry (`POST /api/customer/inquiries`) containing:
  - Chosen catering company (`company_id`).
  - Event title (e.g., "Arun & Priya Wedding Reception").
  - Event type (Wedding, Reception, Birthday, Corporate Gala, Private Function).
  - Event date and guest count.
  - Venue name and address.
  - Dietary requirements (veg, non_veg, mixed, jain, vegan, custom).
  - Special instructions / notes.
- System automatically links or creates a CRM `Customer` record for the caterer, creating the `Event` entity with status `inquiry`.

### FR-CUST-04: Quotation Inspection & Acceptance
- View quotations sent by the caterer (`GET /api/customer/events/{event_id}/quotations`).
- Inspect line items, guest count calculation, tax %, discounts, service charges, delivery charges, and advance payment requirements.
- One-click Quotation Acceptance (`POST /api/customer/events/{event_id}/quotations/{quote_id}/accept`):
  - Sets quotation status to `accepted`.
  - Automatically transitions the event status from `quoted` to `confirmed`.

### FR-CUST-05: Invoices & Payments
- View itemized invoices issued for the event (`GET /api/customer/events/{event_id}/invoices`).
- Review total amount, amount paid, and outstanding balance.
- Record payment / deposit (`POST /api/customer/invoices/{invoice_id}/pay`) with payment method (UPI, Card, Bank Transfer, Net Banking) and transaction reference.
- System automatically updates invoice status (`unpaid` $\to$ `partially_paid` $\to$ `paid`).

### FR-CUST-06: Feedback & Ratings
- Post-event review submission (`POST /api/customer/events/{event_id}/feedback`) with 1-5 star rating and comments.

---

## 3. Data Isolation & Multi-Caterer Interoperability
- A single Customer account can interact with and place bookings with multiple catering businesses independently.
- The customer cannot access internal operational SaaS data of any caterer (workforce wages, inventory stock, profit margins).
